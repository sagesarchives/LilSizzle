# Lil' Sizzle
Lil’ Sizzle is a cooking game that uses simple design and logic, making it ideal for users to navigate the game. Hidden recipe details creates a small challenge for users to complete recipes within Lil’ Sizzle’s kitchen.

# Cook, Bake, Solve!
Choose from two recipes to help Lil’ Sizzle make: Street Corn or Churros!

# Help Lil' Sizzle solve the recipe!
Decode the destroyed recipe card to complete the recipe! Choose the correct ingredients, and complete the meal! <br>
<br>
Don’t choose the wrong ingredients! Choose the wrong ingredient three times and you’ll burn the kitchen!
